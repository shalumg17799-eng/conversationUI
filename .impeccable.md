## Design Context

### Users
Enterprise BI analysts and business stakeholders using a conversational AI interface to generate data reports from BigQuery. Used in office/browser context during work hours. Users need data-dense, scannable layouts — not marketing pages.

### Brand Personality
Warm, editorial, restrained. Think: Notion's warmth × Linear's precision × Perplexity's clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Warm Minimalism** — earthy off-white canvas (`#F7F6F3`), brand accent `#D4572A` (terracotta), generous whitespace, editorial typographic hierarchy. Light mode only. No dark mode, no glassmorphism, no neon. Shadows barely perceptible — depth comes from border + subtle lift.

**Typography:** Bricolage Grotesque (headings/display), Inter (body/labels), JetBrains Mono (metric values/numbers).

**Anti-references:** Cold corporate blue dashboards, heavy drop shadows, gratuitous gradients, rounded "toy" UI, dense enterprise grid layouts.

### Design Principles
1. Data feels like well-typeset editorial content, not a dashboard widget grid
2. Metric values in monospace — numbers deserve their own visual language
3. Headings in Bricolage — distinction between structure and content at a glance
4. Warm tint on every neutral surface — cohesion through brand hue
5. Hover states earn their place — subtle lift and border shift only, no drama
